Context snapshot (all dated, sourced in the sections below): BTC's all-time high was ~$126,080–$126,198 on 6 Oct 2025; the 2026 low was ~$58,000 (30 June / 1 July 2026, ~54% drawdown); a late-August short squeeze lifted price from <$63K to >$81K; the Fed hiked 25 bp to 3.75–4.00% on 16 Sept 2026 (first hike since 2023) and the CLARITY Act failed a Senate cloture vote 49–50 on 15 Sept; BTC dipped to ~$75.5K on 16 Sept, then closed the week of 20 Sept at $81,159 (first weekly close above the 50-week MA in 45 weeks) and squeezed to $84,000–$85,700 on 21 Sept.
