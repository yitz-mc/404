## Key Question 4: What the four-year cycle implies for 2026–2027, and what cycle analysts say now

### Takeaway
By the cycle template the current cycle has behaved almost exactly on schedule: top ~18 months post-halving (Oct 2025, day 1,162 from the prior low vs 1,059 and 1,168 in prior cycles), followed by a ~12-month bear. Cycle analysts (Cowen, Zheng, StoneX) therefore put the durable bottom around Q4 2026 — i.e., *after* the current rally — while noting the 2026 drawdown (~55%) is far shallower than the 78–93% of prior cycles, which either signals a structurally different market (Grayscale, Wintermute, Coin Bureau) or unfinished business (Martinez, CryptoQuant).

### Cited Findings

## Key Question 5: Structural / long-term risks

### Takeaway
Long-tail structural risks have become more concrete in 2026 without becoming near-term: Google-linked research cut the qubit estimate to break secp256k1 by ~20x (≈500K physical qubits, ~9 minutes), NIST deprecates ECC-256 by 2030, and ~6.9M BTC sit in public-key-exposed addresses; but the best-sourced academic estimate still puts a cryptographically relevant quantum computer at ~17% by 2035. Nearer-term structural weaknesses are miner economics (fees at a 10-year low of 0.69% of revenue; record 32K BTC sold by public miners in Q1 2026), custody concentration (~81–84% of US ETF BTC at Coinbase), regulatory stall (CLARITY Act failed 16 Sep 2026) and competition from tokenized equities and stablecoins.

### Cited Findings
**Quantum**
- 2026 research (Google-linked) cut the resource requirement to attack Bitcoin's elliptic-curve cryptography ~20-fold to ~500,000 physical qubits with a runtime of ~9 minutes on a fast-clock superconducting architecture; ~6.9M BTC (~one-third of supply) sit in wallets with exposed public keys, including ~1.7M BTC in early P2PK addresses (Satoshi-era); NIST deprecates ECC-256 by 2030 and disallows it after 2035; Ethereum's Justin Drake assigns ≥10% probability of private-key recovery by 2032 — [The Quantum Insider, 29 Jul 2026](https://thequantuminsider.com/2026/07/29/growing-quantum-security-challenge-bitcoin-digital-assets/); [KuCoin summary](https://www.kucoin.com/news/flash/quantum-computing-threat-to-bitcoin-2026-research-cuts-resource-gap-by-20x)
- "Quantum Horizon" (Gershteyn & Alber, arXiv, 12 Jun 2026): bimodal arrival distribution for a cryptographically relevant QC — ~17% by 2035, ~30% by 2040, ~60% by 2050; ~6M BTC addresses quantum-exposed but only ~2.3M coins face "irreducible" risk, the rest "mostly migratable"; Grover's algorithm does not meaningfully threaten PoW; "the binding constraint is governance, not technology" — [arXiv 2606.14484](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.14484)
- BIP-360 (Pay-to-Merkle-Root, keeps public keys off-chain) merged into the BIPs repo 11 Feb 2026; BIP-361 (April 2026) proposes a three-phase migration including freezing quantum-vulnerable coins and drew a "largely critical" community response; Quantum Safe Bitcoin's hash-based approach costs $75–150 per transaction; a full fix may take 5–7 years — [Yahoo/The Block](https://finance.yahoo.com/news/bitcoin-developers-merge-bip-360-230742889.html); [KuCoin BIP-361](https://www.kucoin.com/blog/bip-361-explained-bitcoin-new-plan-to-survive-quantum-computing); [Quantum Insider](https://thequantuminsider.com/2026/07/29/growing-quantum-security-challenge-bitcoin-digital-assets/)

**Miner economics / fee sustainability**
- Transaction fees are 0.69% of miner revenue, lowest share in 10 years (Glassnode's fee-share hit 0.52% in April 2026, 2016 levels); subsidy is 3.125 BTC/block, halving to 1.5625 BTC in 2028; researchers argue a fee-only security model is unstable because fees are variable, creating windows where attacks are "temporarily cheap" — [GNCrypto](https://www.gncrypto.news/news/bitcoin-miner-fees-0-69-lowest-in-10-years/); [The Block](https://www.theblock.co/post/379291/bitcoin-miner-fees-fall-12-month-low-underscoring-long-term-reliance-block-subsidies); [SSRN: Lim, "Bitcoin's Security Budget at the Subsidy-to-Fee Transition"](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/Delivery.cfm/6816419.pdf?abstractid=6816419&mirid=1)
- Public miners sold >32,000 BTC in Q1 2026, a single-quarter record exceeding all of 2025 and the ~20K BTC sold during the 2022 Terra collapse; hashprice fell to $28–30/PH/day in Q1 2026 and ~$29 by July 2026, below the ~$35 breakeven for older machines; further capitulation of high-cost operators expected absent a price recovery — [CoinShares Q1 2026 mining report](https://coinshares.com/insights/research-data/bitcoin-mining-report-q1-2026/); [Lumerin May 2026](https://medium.com/lumerin-blog/the-current-state-of-bitcoin-mining-may-2026-d75e5b50dda5); [CoinDesk 25 Feb 2026](https://www.coindesk.com/markets/2026/02/25/one-of-longest-mining-capitulations-nears-end-signaling-potential-btc-price-bottom)
- Difficulty dropped ~20% from peak in a confirmed miner capitulation — [Coingabbar](https://www.coingabbar.com/en/crypto-currency-news/bitcoin-miner-capitulation-difficulty-drops-20-percent) (secondary source); MARA and Bitdeer are diverting BTC to AI infrastructure — [CoinDesk 24 Jul 2026](https://www.coindesk.com/markets/2026/07/24/bitcoin-treasury-companies-sell-up-repay-debt-pivot-to-ai-as-share-prices-collapse)

**Regulatory reversal / political risk**
- The CLARITY Act (market-structure bill) failed a 60-vote cloture motion in the Senate on 16 Sep 2026 with all voting Democrats opposed; Galaxy's Novogratz said government "feels broken" after 18 months of negotiation "derailed by election-year politics" — [U.Today](https://u.today/peter-schiff-says-bitcoin-is-worthless-after-clarity-failure); [Coinpedia](https://coinpedia.org/news/fomc-meeting-today-live-updates-btc-eth-xrp-fall-as-clarity-act-fails-rate-hike-looms/)
- The US Strategic Bitcoin Reserve exists only by executive order (6 Mar 2025); "a future president can reverse an executive order"; holdings ~198,000 BTC unaudited (broader federal holdings est. ~328,372 BTC); the May 2026 Begich/Golden bill dropped the 1M-BTC purchase target and added a 20-year lockup, after which Treasury could recommend selling up to 10% per two-year period — [The Block 22 May 2026](https://www.theblock.co/news/regulation/2026-05-22-new-strategic-bitcoin-reserve-bill-drops-btc-purchase-target-adds-lockup-402264); [24/7 Wall St 15 Sep 2026](https://247wallst.com/investing/cryptocurrency/2026/09/15/what-is-the-strategic-bitcoin-reserve-bill-what-wednesdays-house-vote-means-for-bitcoin/); [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/U.S._Strategic_Bitcoin_Reserve)

- As of 8 Apr 2026, US BTC ETF AUM was $91.71B and funds naming Coinbase as custodian held ~$77.1B (84.1%; stricter count $74.06B / 80.8%); 10 of 12 spot ETFs use Coinbase-related custody (Fidelity self-custodies; VanEck uses Gemini); an outage, settlement bottleneck or enforcement action at Coinbase "could disrupt creation/redemption across ETFs" — [CryptoSlate](https://cryptoslate.com/over-80-of-bitcoin-etf-assets-hit-coinbase-custody-choke-point-with-74b-at-risk/); Coinbase reported a $667M loss raising the "1.5M BTC question" — [CryptoSlate](https://cryptoslate.com/are-spot-bitcoin-etfs-at-risk-after-custodian-coinbase-reports-667m-loss-the-1-5m-btc-question/)

### Inferences
- Quantum is a speculative long-tail risk on a 2030s horizon, but its *market* risk could arrive earlier via narrative: a credible demonstration (e.g., breaking a small-curve key) or a contentious BIP-361-style freeze debate could hit price and governance long before any actual theft. The ~1.7M BTC in P2PK/Satoshi-era addresses that cannot be migrated by anyone is the most concrete "irreducible" exposure.
- Miner stress is a *near-term, high-probability* pressure: with fees at ~0.7% of revenue and hashprice below older-rig breakeven, miners are structural sellers into any weakness, and the 2028 halving mechanically halves the security budget again.
- Regulatory risk has flipped from tailwind to two-sided: with CLARITY dead before the Nov 2026 midterms, the executive-order-based reserve and SEC exemptions are reversible by the next administration.
